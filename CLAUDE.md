## Backtest conventions (from the owner)

- Margin per rung (one short straddle or strangle, 1 lot per leg, NIFTY) is about Rs 1.5 lakh; straddle and strangle are almost the same.
- Capital for a staggered strategy = number of rungs x Rs 1.5 lakh. Example: 09:20-13:00 every minute = 221 rungs = Rs 3.315 crore.
